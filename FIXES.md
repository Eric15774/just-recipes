# Fixes Needed

A running list of gaps and errors in the original recipe text from the old
Google Sites version. These come from the source recipes, not from the
conversion. Each item is also noted in the recipe's own "Notes" section, so
you can fix things here or directly in the files.

Recipe files live in `src/recipes/`.

## Missing amounts or ingredients

- **Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies** (`oatmeal-chocolate-chip-cookies.md`): no butter amount. Similar recipes use about 1 cup (2 sticks), but that is a guess.
- **Minnie's Brownies** (`minnies-brownies.md`): no egg count or salt amount.
- **Three Cup Sugar Peanut Butter Fudge** (`three-cup-sugar-peanut-butter-fudge.md`): the sugar line was missing. 3 cups was used from the title. Confirm.
- **30 Minute Dinner Rolls** (`30-minute-dinner-rolls.md`): directions call for salt, but no amount is listed.
- **Cream Potatoes and Cheese** (`cream-potatoes-and-cheese.md`): flour has no amount.
- **Frog Eye Salad** (`frog-eye-salad.md`): acini di pepe has no amount.
- **Cranberry Jello Salad** (`cranberry-jello-salad.md`): no water amount (box directions used).
- **Quinoa Salad** (`quinoa-salad.md`): unclear whether quinoa is measured cooked or dry, and no cooking instructions.
- **Ribbon Salad** (`ribbon-salad.md`): "1 can crushed pineapple juice" is unclear, and the can size is missing.
- **Pickle Chips** (`pickle-chips.md`): no number of cucumbers, brine strength, or water amounts for the alum and ginger boils.
- **Crock Pot Rice** (`crock-pot-rice.md`): unclear line about cooking longer on high and the water amount.

## Missing times or temperatures

- **Carob Peanut Butter Bars** (`carob-peanut-butter-bars.md`): no temperature or time for toasting the oats. Title was spelled "Carab" in the original.
- **Communion Bread, Conventional** (`communion-bread-conventional.md`): only "a slow oven until done."
- **Florence's Rolls** (`florences-rolls.md`): no baking time.
- **New Dinner Rolls** (`new-dinner-rolls.md`): no baking time. The warming step says to microwave the milk for "10 minutes," probably a typo. Also, quantities were decimals (0.3 cups, 2.3 teaspoons) and were converted to 1/3 cup and 1 packet of yeast. Confirm.
- **Meatballs** (`meatballs.md`): no cooking time.
- **Strawberry Jello Pretzel Salad** (`strawberry-jello-pretzel-salad.md`): no crust baking time, only "until toasted."
- **Favorite Pizza Dough** (`favorite-pizza-dough.md`): no bake time or pan size.

## Errors or inconsistencies

- **Hefezopf** (`hefezopf.md`): directions say mix the yeast with milk, but the list has warm water and no milk for the dough. Warm water was used.
- **Ripe Tomato Relish** (`ripe-tomato-relish.md`): the title says ripe, but the ingredients list green tomatoes.
- **Warm Molcajete Salsa** (`warm-molcajete-salsa.md`): cilantro was "0.3 cups," converted to 1/3 cup.

## Incomplete or outline-only recipes

- **White Chocolate Raspberry Cheesecake** (`white-chocolate-raspberry-cheesecake.md`): the original cuts off after the crust. The filling and baking steps were never written down.
- **Chiles en Nogada** (`chiles-en-nogada.md`): outline only, no amounts or steps.
- **Mole Poblano** (`mole-poblano.md`): outline only, no amounts or steps.

## Other to-dos

## How to use this

Work through the list top to bottom, fixing each recipe from memory, an old
physical copy, or by deciding on reasonable amounts, before the site goes
live. Delete the Notes line in the recipe once fixed.

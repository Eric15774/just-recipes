## Ingredients

Filling (cook until thick):

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup King Syrup
- 1 egg, beaten
- 4 tbsp flour
- 2 cups water
- 2 tsp vanilla

Crumb mixture:

- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1/4 cup shortening
- 1 tsp soda
- 1 tsp cream of tartar

## Directions

1. Cook the filling ingredients together until thick. Cool.
2. Mix the crumb mixture ingredients into crumbs.
3. Pour the cooled filling into 2 unbaked 9-inch pie shells and cover with the crumb mixture.
4. Bake in a 350°F oven for 40 to 45 minutes.

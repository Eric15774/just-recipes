## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Butter
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- Brown sugar
- 2/3 cup butter flavored Crisco
- 2/3 cup milk
- Cinnamon
- 6 apples, peeled and cored

## Directions

1. Blend all dry ingredients (except brown sugar and cinnamon). Cut in the Crisco.
2. Add the milk and knead until a smooth dough forms.
3. Roll the dough 1/4 inch thick and cut into 6 even pieces.
4. For each dumpling, wrap an apple, 1 tsp brown sugar, and a pat of butter in a piece of dough.
5. Place the dumplings in a shallow baking dish and sprinkle each with cinnamon.
6. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes.

## Notes

- Can serve with ice cream or whipped cream.

## Ingredients

Dough:

- 2 1/2 sticks butter
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 2 1/2 cups flour
- 1 1/2 jars Baker's Apricot Filling (or filling of choice)

Icing (makes more than needed):

- 1 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 8 oz. cream cheese, softened
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla

## Directions

1. Combine the butter, sugar, egg, vanilla, and flour with a mixer to form a dough. Chill 1 hour.
2. Line tassie pans with the dough. Fill each dough cup 3/4 full with the apricot filling.
3. Bake 15 minutes at 350°F. Cool in the pan 10 minutes.
4. Mix the icing ingredients well and spread over the top of each cup once cool.

## Notes

- They freeze well if you need them for later.

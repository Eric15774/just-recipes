## Ingredients

- 3 cups whole white wheat flour
- 1/4 cup raw sugar
- 1/4 cup golden flax meal
- 1 Tbsp aluminum free baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp nutmeg
- 1 cup soymilk
- 1/2 Tbsp lemon juice
- 2 cups mashed overripe bananas (about 4 large)
- 1/3 cup light olive oil
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts, optional

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Spray the muffin tins (I use regular size and 1 baby size).
2. Mix the dry ingredients. Mix the liquids. Stir together thoroughly.
3. Fill the muffin cups about 2/3 full.
4. Bake 18-20 minutes. Cool in the tins 5 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack for 5 minutes.

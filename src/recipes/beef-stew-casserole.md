## Ingredients

- 1 or 2 cans beef stew
- Bisquick mix
- Milk

## Directions

1. Put beef stew into a casserole dish. Preheat oven to 375 degrees.
2. Mix drop biscuits according to the directions on the Bisquick box. Drop over the beef stew.
3. Bake until biscuits are browned and the stew is hot and bubbly.

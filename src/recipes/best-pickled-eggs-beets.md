## Ingredients

- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 1/4 cups sugar
- 1 cup vinegar
- 1 pound can beets, sliced or quartered
- 12-14 hard boiled eggs

## Directions

1. Stir the salt, sugar, vinegar, and beets together in a saucepan. Bring to a boil.
2. Pour over the peeled hard boiled eggs. Refrigerate.

## Notes

- I use this same recipe for making pickled beets.

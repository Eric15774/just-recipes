## Ingredients

- 2 sticks butter (straight from fridge)
- 3 cups flour
- 3/4 cup white sugar
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 3 cups chocolate chips
- 2 tsp vanilla

## Directions

1. Mix the butter and sugars well. Add the eggs and vanilla and mix well.
2. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt. Stir lightly before mixing well.
3. Add the chocolate chips and mix well.
4. Scoop and bake on parchment-lined paper at 375°F for 9-11 minutes.

## Notes

- I use my stand mixer for all mixing and just a spoon to scoop.
- If making M&M cookies, use 1 1/2 cup chocolate chips and 1 1/2 cup M&Ms.

## Ingredients

- 3 (15 oz) cans black beans, well rinsed
- 1 1/2 cups cooked corn (or 1 bag frozen)
- 2 medium tomatoes, diced
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 1 green pepper, chopped
- 1 orange pepper, chopped
- 1 yellow pepper, chopped
- 1 red onion, diced
- 1-2 jalapeño peppers with seeds, chopped
- 1/2 cup lime juice
- 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp ground red chile or cayenne pepper

## Directions

1. Chop and mix everything together.
2. Serve with tortilla chips or on a salad.

## Notes

- You can eliminate a pepper, or just use half of each color of pepper.
- Will keep about five days in the refrigerator. Makes a lot!

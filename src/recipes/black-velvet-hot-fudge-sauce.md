## Ingredients

- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1 Tbsp light corn syrup
- 1/4 cup cocoa powder
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup semi-sweet/dark chocolate chips

## Directions

1. In a saucepan, combine the sugar, milk, butter, syrup, cocoa, and chips.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture boils. Reduce heat to low and continue to cook for 8 minutes, stirring constantly.
3. Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla.
4. Serve warm over ice cream, or use as a dip for fruit.

## Notes

- Keeps well if covered and refrigerated, and can be reheated in the microwave.

A craft recipe for decorations, not for eating.

## Ingredients

- 2 parts flour
- 1 part salt
- 1 part water
- A few drops of dish detergent
- Paper clips
- Food coloring (optional, to pre-color the dough)
- Beads for eyes or other bake-safe decorations (optional)
- Water-based paint, acrylic spray, and polyurethane, for finishing

## Directions

1. Put a few drops of dish detergent into the water, then combine all the ingredients. Knead until well mixed.
2. Form into shapes, or roll and cut out with cookie cutters.
3. Place a small paper clip into the top, leaving a little sticking out for the hanger. Add beads for eyes or anything else that is okay to bake.
4. Bake at 325°F, 1/2 hour for every 1/4 inch of dough thickness. You can bake longer at a lower setting if you are afraid of browning the dough.
5. After baking and cooling, paint with any water-based paint. Seal with acrylic spray and polyurethane.

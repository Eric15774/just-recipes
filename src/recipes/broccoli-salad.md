## Ingredients

- 8 cups fresh broccoli, cut into bite sized pieces
- 1/2 red onion, cut into thin bite sized pieces
- 1/2 pound bacon, cooked and crumbled
- 3/4 cup raisins or craisins
- 3/4 cup sliced almonds, toasted
- 4 oz cheddar cheese, cubed
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 3 Tbsp red wine vinegar
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Combine the broccoli, red onion, bacon, raisins or craisins, almonds, and cheese in a large bowl.
2. Prepare the dressing: mix the mayo, sugar, and vinegar together until smooth. Stir into the salad.
3. Chill for 3 hours. Season with salt and pepper and serve.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups shredded chicken breast
- 2 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup Ranch dressing
- 3/4 cup pepper sauce (such as Frank's Red Hot)
- 1 1/2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese

## Directions

1. Spray a small crockpot with cooking spray.
2. Place all the ingredients in and turn on low. Stir occasionally until the cheese is melted and the mixture is warmed through.
3. Serve with veggies, tortilla chips, or crackers.

## Ingredients

- 420 grams bread flour (about 3 1/4 cups)
- 300 ml buttermilk (about 1 1/4 cups), warmed to about 100°F
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 2 tablespoons honey or sugar
- 2 1/4 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 tablespoon butter, melted, for brushing the top

## Directions

1. Gently warm the buttermilk to about 100°F. It may look a little curdled; that is fine. Stir in the melted butter and the honey or sugar.
2. In a large bowl, whisk the flour, yeast, salt, and baking soda. Pour in the buttermilk mixture and stir until a shaggy dough forms.
3. Knead by hand 8-10 minutes, or 6 minutes in a stand mixer with a dough hook, until smooth and elastic. The dough should be slightly tacky but not sticky.
4. First rise: place in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot until doubled, about 60-90 minutes.
5. Shape: press the dough into a rectangle about 9 inches wide. Roll it up tightly from the short side, pinch the seam closed, and set it seam side down in a greased 9x5 loaf pan.
6. Second rise: cover and let rise until the dough crowns about 1 inch above the rim of the pan, 45-60 minutes. Preheat the oven to 350°F during the last 20 minutes.
7. Bake 35-40 minutes, until deep golden and the inside reads 190°F. Tent with foil after 20 minutes if the top browns too fast.
8. Turn out of the pan, brush the top with melted butter for a soft crust, and cool on a rack at least 1 hour before slicing.

## Notes

- Bread flour gives the best rise and structure. All-purpose works, but the loaf will be a bit softer and lower.
- The baking soda tames the tang and helps browning. Leave it out if you like a more sour flavor.
- No buttermilk: stir 1 tablespoon lemon juice or vinegar into regular milk and let it sit 5 minutes.
- Keeps 3-4 days wrapped at room temperature, or freeze it sliced.

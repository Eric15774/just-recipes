## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 sticks butter
- 3 cups packed brown sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 2 1/4 cups flour
- 1 cup chopped nuts
- 2 1/4 tsp maple flavoring

## Directions

1. Cream the butter and brown sugar. Add the eggs one at a time.
2. Add the baking powder, flour, nuts, and maple flavoring.
3. Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes.

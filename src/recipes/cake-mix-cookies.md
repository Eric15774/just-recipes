## Ingredients

- 1 cake mix, any flavor
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Mix all ingredients together until completely blended.
3. Scoop the cookie batter and place on a parchment-lined baking sheet, at least 2 inches apart.
4. Bake for 10-12 minutes depending on desired crispiness.
5. Remove from oven and allow to cool 5-7 minutes on the baking sheet before transferring to a wire cooling rack.

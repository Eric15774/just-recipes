## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 8 oz Velveeta cheese
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 2 lbs powdered sugar
- 1/2 cup cocoa

## Directions

1. Melt the butter and cheese over low heat.
2. Take off heat and stir in the vanilla, powdered sugar, and cocoa that has been blended together.
3. Mix quickly and spread in a buttered 9x13 pan.
4. Let stand for 2 hours before serving.

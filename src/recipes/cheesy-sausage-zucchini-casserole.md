## Ingredients

- 1 package sausage (links or ground)
- 1 can mushrooms, or sliced fresh mushrooms
- 1 medium zucchini, cubed
- 1 can diced tomatoes (Rotel for a little spice)
- 1/4 cup chopped onion
- 1-1 1/2 cups uncooked pasta
- Shredded cheese
- Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper

## Directions

1. Cook the pasta.
2. Cook sausage and onion in a large skillet until browned. Drain excess grease. Stir in zucchini and tomatoes and cook until tender.
3. In a greased 9x13 pan, mix the sausage, zucchini, and other vegetables. Add shredded cheese and spices to taste.
4. Bake uncovered for 40 minutes at 325°F, or until browned on top.

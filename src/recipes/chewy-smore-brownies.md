## Ingredients

- 1 21 oz package brownie mix (plus ingredients called for on the box)
- 2 cups miniature marshmallows
- Graham crackers, broken into quarters (about 35 quarters for a 13x9 pan)
- 1 cup milk chocolate or semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease the bottom of a 13x9 pan. Prepare and bake the brownie mix according to the directions on the box.
2. Immediately upon removal from the oven, sprinkle the marshmallows and chocolate chips over the warm brownie surface. Return to the oven for 3 or 4 minutes to melt.
3. Remove from the oven and arrange the graham crackers on top of the hot marshmallows, pressing down lightly.
4. Cool completely and cut into servings around the graham crackers.

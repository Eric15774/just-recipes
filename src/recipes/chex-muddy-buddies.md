## Ingredients

- 9 cups Chex cereal (any variety)
- 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 1/2 cup powdered sugar

## Directions

1. Measure the cereal into a large bowl and set aside.
2. Heat the chips, peanut butter, and butter in a saucepan over low heat, stirring frequently, until melted. Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla.
3. Pour the chocolate mixture over the cereal and gently stir until evenly coated.
4. Put into a large Ziploc bag and add the powdered sugar. Seal the bag and shake until well coated.
5. Spread onto waxed paper to cool.

## Notes

- Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

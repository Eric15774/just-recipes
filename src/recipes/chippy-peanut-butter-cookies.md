## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter, softened
- 1 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 1/4 cups flour
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 package peanut butter chips
- 1 package milk chocolate chips

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, cream the butter, peanut butter, and sugars until light and fluffy. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
2. Combine the flour, baking soda, and salt; gradually add to the creamed mixture and mix well.
3. Stir in the chips.
4. Scoop and place on a cookie sheet.
5. Bake at 350°F for 12-15 minutes. Cool.

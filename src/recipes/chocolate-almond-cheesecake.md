## Ingredients

Crust:

- 1 c. chocolate graham cracker crumbs
- 1/4 c. melted butter
- 1/4 c. chopped almonds
- 3 tbsp. brown sugar

Cake:

- 1 1/2 c. chocolate chips
- 3 (8 oz.) cream cheese
- 3/4 c. brown sugar
- 3 eggs
- 3 tbsp. milk
- 3/4 tsp. almond extract (can also use vanilla extract)

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Mix together the crust ingredients and press into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan (bottom of pan may be lined with parchment paper). Bake 10-15 min.
2. Melt the chocolate chips in a heavy saucepan, stirring until smooth, and set aside.
3. Beat the cream cheese until smooth. Mix in the brown sugar, eggs, milk, and extract. Mix in the melted chocolate.
4. Pour the batter over the cooled crust. Bake 35-40 min.
5. Cool on a cooling rack and refrigerate to cool completely. Run a knife around the edge of the cheesecake before removing the sides of the pan.

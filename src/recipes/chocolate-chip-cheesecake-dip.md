## Ingredients

- 1 8 oz package cream cheese
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 3/4 cup confectioners' sugar
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 package mini chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Beat the cream cheese and butter until light and fluffy.
2. Add the sugar and vanilla; beat until smooth.
3. Stir in the chocolate chips. Chill.

## Notes

- Serve with graham crackers, pretzels, or vanilla wafers.

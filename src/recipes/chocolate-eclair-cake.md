## Ingredients

- 3 layers graham crackers
- 2 boxes French vanilla pudding
- 1 carton Cool Whip
- 2 1/2 cup milk
- 1 can chocolate icing

## Directions

1. Mix the pudding, milk, and Cool Whip.
2. Layer graham crackers, half of the pudding mixture, another layer of graham crackers, the rest of the pudding mixture, and a final layer of graham crackers.
3. Refrigerate overnight.
4. Top with the frosting before serving.

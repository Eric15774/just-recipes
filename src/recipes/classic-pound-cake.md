The traditional pound cake was so named for the amount of its constituent ingredients: there should be one pound each of butter, eggs, sugar, and flour.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (4 sticks) unsalted butter, room temp.
- 2 1/4 cups sugar
- 6 large eggs, room temp.
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 4 cups sifted all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp vanilla extract, or almond extract, optional
- Butter and flour to prep the pan

## Directions

1. Beat the butter on low until lightly creamed (30 sec). While the mixer is on low, gradually add the sugar. Beat until creamed and fluffy (2 min).
2. Scrape the sides of the bowl and add the eggs, 1 at a time, beating well after each egg is added.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the flour and salt.
4. Add the creamed mixture to the dry mix in thirds, beating on low each time during the addition.
5. When all is mixed, fold in the optional extract if using. Pour into a lightly greased and floured 10-inch tube pan and smooth the top.
6. Bake at 325°F on the center rack until the top is golden brown and firm to the touch, about 1 hour 20-25 min.
7. Remove and cool in the pan on a wire rack for 20 min. Run a knife around the pan edge to release the cake. Cool right side up on a wire rack for 1 hour before slicing.

## Notes

- Even with a traditional pound cake, add a tablespoon or two of vanilla before the last addition of flour.
- I usually bake this in a loaf pan and tent it with foil for the first hour. Total baking time at 325°F is probably an hour and a half, depending on your oven.

## Ingredients

- 2 pkg chocolate chip cookie dough
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 (8 oz) pkg cream cheese

## Directions

1. Place a layer of one package of cookie dough on the bottom of a cake pan. Press together.
2. Mix the eggs, sugar, and cream cheese and pour over the cookie dough layer.
3. Slice the other package of cookie dough and place on top of the mixture.
4. Bake at 350°F for 1/2 hour, or until golden brown.

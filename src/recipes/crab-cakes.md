## Ingredients

- 2 cups grated zucchini (skin on)
- 1 egg
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 Tbsp mayo
- 1 tsp Old Bay Seasoning

## Directions

1. Mix all ingredients together.
2. Form into patties.
3. Fry in a small amount of oil until brown.

## Notes

- May be served with red hot sauce or catsup for dipping. Ranch dip or dressing also works well.

## Ingredients

- 1 small box (3 oz) raspberry jello
- 1 can cranberry sauce
- 1 can crushed pineapple, drained
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

## Directions

1. Make the jello and let it thicken.
2. Mix the cranberry sauce and the drained pineapple into the jello, then add the walnuts.
3. Put back in the refrigerator to set up.

## Notes

- The original does not say how much water to use for the jello. Use the box directions.

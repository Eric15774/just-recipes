## Ingredients

- 1 package (30 oz) frozen shredded southern style hash brown potatoes, thawed
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup butter or margarine, melted
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- 16 oz sour cream
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 8 oz shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 can (10 3/4 oz) condensed cream of chicken soup, undiluted

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients. Transfer to an ungreased 9x13 baking dish.
2. Bake, uncovered, at 350°F for 1 hour, or until heated through.

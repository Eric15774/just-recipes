## Ingredients

Base:

- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup butter
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 (16 oz.) can Hershey's syrup

Mint layer:

- 2 cups powdered (10x) sugar
- 1 stick butter
- 4 tbsp. green crème de menthe

Chocolate layer:

- 1 cup chocolate chips
- 6 tbsp. butter

## Directions

1. Cream the sugar and 1/2 cup butter. Add the eggs, flour, vanilla, salt, and Hershey's syrup.
2. Pour into a greased and floured 9x13 pan. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes. Cool.
3. Mix and stir the mint layer ingredients until smooth. Spread on the cooled cake.
4. Melt the chocolate layer ingredients together in a double boiler. Cool, then spread on top.
5. Cut into small bars.

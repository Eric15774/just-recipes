## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice, either brown or white
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp imitation butter flavor, optional
- 4-5 cups water

## Directions

1. Make in a 2 quart crock pot.
2. Cook on low 4-6 hours.

## Notes

- Eat with beans, stir fry, or just sprinkle with salt and eat.
- Used in [Haystacks](/recipes/haystacks/).
- The original has an unclear line about cooking longer on high and the amount of water.

## Ingredients

- 1 (8 oz.) pkg. cream cheese, softened
- 1 c. crunchy peanut butter
- 1 c. milk
- 2 c. powdered sugar
- 1 (12 oz.) whipped topping
- 2 chocolate pie crusts
- Chocolate syrup

## Directions

1. Beat together the cream cheese, peanut butter, milk, and powdered sugar.
2. Fold in the whipped topping.
3. Fill the pie crusts.
4. Drizzle with chocolate syrup and freeze.

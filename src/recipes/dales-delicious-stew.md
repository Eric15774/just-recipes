## Ingredients

- 4-5 large potatoes
- 5 medium carrots
- 1 medium onion
- 1 large green bell pepper
- 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 1 (14 oz) can water
- 1 1/2 t. salt (can cut way back)
- 1 t. garlic powder
- 1 t. oregano
- 1 t. celery salt
- 1 t. cumin
- 1 t. Italian seasoning
- 1 t. parsley
- 1/4 t. pepper, optional
- 1 T. honey
- 1 1/2 t. olive oil

## Directions

1. Dice the potatoes, just cover with water, bring to a boil, then turn to medium-high. Cook about 10 minutes.
2. Add the sliced carrots and cook about 5 minutes.
3. Add the diced onion and bell pepper and cook about 3-5 minutes.
4. Add all the rest and simmer at least 15 minutes.

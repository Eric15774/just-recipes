## Ingredients

- 6 hardboiled eggs
- 1/4 cup Miracle Whip (or mayo)
- 1 tsp vinegar
- 1 tsp prepared mustard
- Paprika or salt, for the top

## Directions

1. Carefully peel the hardboiled eggs and cut them lengthwise in half.
2. Gently scoop out the yellow part into a bowl and mash with a fork.
3. In a separate bowl, mix the Miracle Whip, vinegar, and mustard. Stir the wet mix into the yolks until smooth.
4. Evenly spoon the mixture into the dips in the whites.

## Notes

- Some people sprinkle paprika on top to make them fancy, but I like them with a little salt on top.

## Ingredients

- 1 can crushed pineapple
- 1 box cherry jello
- 1 pt cottage cheese
- 1 container Cool Whip

## Directions

1. Put the pineapple in a pan and bring to a boil.
2. Add the cherry jello and mix until dissolved.
3. Remove from the stove and let cool.
4. Add the cottage cheese and Cool Whip. Chill and serve.

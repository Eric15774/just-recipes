## Ingredients

- 1 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Cream the peanut butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Stir in the egg, vanilla, and chocolate chips.
2. Drop by heaping teaspoon 2 inches apart onto a lightly greased cookie sheet. Flatten cookies slightly with a fork.
3. Bake at 350°F for 15 to 20 minutes, or until set. Remove to a wire rack to cool.

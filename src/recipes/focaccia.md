Uses one portion of [Hope's Current Bread Dough](/recipes/hopes-current-bread-dough/).

## Ingredients

- 1 portion Hope's Current Bread Dough
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp basil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (roughly)
- 1 can natural olives, sliced
- 1 can stewed tomatoes, drained

## Directions

1. Roll out the dough on a sprayed cookie sheet, or in a 9x13 pan for a "deep dish."
2. Mix the olive oil, basil, salt, and garlic and spread across the top of the dough.
3. Add the olives and tomatoes and spread evenly.
4. Raise in a warm oven (on the lowest setting) for about 20 minutes.
5. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes. Serve hot or cold.

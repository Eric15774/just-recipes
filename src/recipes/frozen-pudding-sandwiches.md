## Ingredients

- 1 pkg. (4 serving size) instant pudding pie filling, vanilla or chocolate
- 1 1/2 cups cold milk
- 1 1/2 cups peanut butter
- 1 box graham crackers

## Directions

1. Slowly mix the milk and peanut butter in a mixing bowl.
2. Add the pudding and beat slowly for 1-2 minutes. Let stand for 3-5 minutes.
3. Break graham crackers in half and spread the pudding mixture on a sandwich, about 1/2 inch thick.
4. Freeze.

## Notes

- I usually double this recipe to make about 40 sandwiches.

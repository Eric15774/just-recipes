## Ingredients

- 1 (24 oz) carton small curd cottage cheese
- 2 (3 oz) packages orange jello
- 1 (8 oz) carton Cool Whip
- 1 (11 oz) can mandarin oranges, drained
- 1 (16 oz) can crushed pineapple, drained

## Directions

1. Mix the cottage cheese, jello, and Cool Whip.
2. Fold in the oranges and crushed pineapple.
3. Pour into a container and chill.

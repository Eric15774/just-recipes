## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, optional
- 2 eggs
- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- Oil, for frying
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

## Directions

1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon in a bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, and vanilla.
3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients until you get a smooth, pourable batter, a bit thinner than pancake batter.
4. Heat about an inch and a half of oil in a deep pan to 375°F.
5. Pour the batter into the oil in a swirling, weaving pattern, either straight from a measuring cup or through a funnel.
6. Fry for 2-3 minutes until golden, flip once, then drain on paper towels and dust generously with powdered sugar while still warm.

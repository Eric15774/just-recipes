## Ingredients

- 1 chocolate cake mix
- 1 small chocolate instant pudding
- 1/4 cup oil
- 2 1/2 cups milk, divided
- 4 eggs
- 2 Tbsp flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup shortening
- 1 stick oleo
- Powdered sugar

## Directions

1. Combine the cake mix, pudding mix, oil, eggs, and 1 1/2 cups of the milk. Mix well.
2. Pour the batter into two 10x15x1 pans lined with parchment paper. Bake at 325°F for 15-20 minutes.
3. Combine the flour and 1 cup of the milk. Cook until the mixture thickens, then let cool.
4. Cream the sugar, shortening, and oleo together. Combine the creamed mixture with the cooled flour mixture.
5. Spread the filling onto one of the cooled baked cakes. Gently place the other cake on top.

## Notes

- Can sprinkle powdered sugar on top, or cut into rectangles and wrap individually for serving.

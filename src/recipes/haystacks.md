A build-your-own plate. Stack whatever you like.

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice (see [Crock Pot Rice](/recipes/crock-pot-rice/))
- Cooked pinto beans (dry ones cooked in a crockpot taste best)
- Shredded lettuce
- Diced tomatoes
- Sliced olives
- Diced onions
- Diced bell peppers
- Guacamole or diced avocado
- Ranch type dressing (we like [Creamy Vegan Italian Dressing](/recipes/creamy-vegan-italian-dressing/))
- Tortilla chips

## Directions

1. Stack the foods you want on your plate and serve with tortilla chips.

## Ingredients

- 1 box angel food cake mix
- 1 20 oz can crushed pineapple

## Directions

1. Mix the cake mix and pineapple together, using the juice as well, no need to add the other cake mix ingredients.
2. Mix with a spoon; something "magical" happens when you do.
3. Place in a 9x13 pan and bake at 350°F for 30 minutes on the lower rack of the oven.

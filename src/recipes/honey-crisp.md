## Ingredients

- 3 cups apple sauce
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp oil
- 1/4 cup oats
- Apple chunks, optional

## Directions

1. Mix all ingredients in a bowl.
2. Pour into a pie dish.
3. Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes or so.

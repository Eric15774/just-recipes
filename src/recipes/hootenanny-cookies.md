## Ingredients

- 1 cup oleo, soft
- 1 cup light brown sugar
- 2 eggs, slightly beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3 cups sifted flour
- 1/2 tsp soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup hot water
- 1 cup each dates, raisins, and nuts

## Directions

1. Cream the oleo, add the sugar, vanilla, and eggs. Beat until light.
2. Add the sifted dry ingredients alternately with the water. Mix well.
3. Stir in the fruits and nuts.
4. Drop by teaspoon onto a greased cookie sheet and bake in a preheated 325°F oven for 10-12 minutes.

## Notes

- Store in an airtight container.

## Ingredients

- 1 gallon apple cider
- 4 cinnamon sticks
- 4 whole allspice
- 4 cloves
- 2 orange slices

## Directions

1. Put the spices in a cheesecloth.
2. Add the spice bundle, orange slices, and apple cider to a stockpot.
3. Heat until it boils.

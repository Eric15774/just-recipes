## Ingredients

- 1135 grams (2 1/2 pounds) flour
- 300 grams (1 1/4 cups) powdered milk
- 72 grams (3/8 cup) double-acting baking powder
- 28 grams (1 1/2 Tbs) salt
- 11 grams (1 Tbs) cream of tartar
- 50 grams (1/4 cup) sugar
- 1 lb shortening


## Directions

1. Whisk together dry ingredients
2. Cut in shortening
3. Store at room temperature unless using whole grain flour. In that case refrigerate.

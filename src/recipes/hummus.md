## Ingredients

- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed (cooked chickpeas work if that is what you have)
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 cup [tahini](/recipes/tahini/) (stir it well)
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- Salt, to taste
- 2-3 tablespoons water

## Directions

1. Combine the chickpeas, lemon juice, tahini, garlic, olive oil, and cumin in a food processor.
2. Blend until smooth, about 1-2 minutes. If the mixture is too thick, add water one tablespoon at a time while blending.
3. Taste and add salt as needed.
4. Serve with a drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkle of paprika if desired.

## Notes

- For smoother hummus, you can peel the chickpeas before blending.

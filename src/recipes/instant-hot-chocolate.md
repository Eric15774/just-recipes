## Ingredients

- 1 large box powdered milk
- 1 large jar Cremora
- 1 large box quick chocolate
- 1 lb confectioners' sugar
- Miniature marshmallows

## Directions

1. Mix all ingredients together and place in an airtight container.
2. To serve, mix 1/4 cup of the mix with hot water. Enjoy!

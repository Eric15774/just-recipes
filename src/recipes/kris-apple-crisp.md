## Ingredients

- 2 cups quick oats
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 12 tablespoons butter, softened
- Apples, enough to fill the baking dish

## Directions

1. Cream together the butter and the sugars. Then mix in the flour and oats.
2. Peel, core, and slice enough apples to fill the baking dish.
3. Spread the crumble on top of the apples.
4. Bake at 350°F for approximately 45 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 3/4 cup white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp soda
- 3 cups flour
- 1 package chocolate chips
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions

1. Combine the butter, sugars, and eggs. Add the soda and flour. Lastly, add the chocolate chips and vanilla.
2. Drop onto cookie sheets. Bake 10 to 12 minutes at 370°F.

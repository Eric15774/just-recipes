## Ingredients

- 1 box yellow or white cake mix
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 2 cups chocolate chips
- 1 stick melted butter

## Directions

1. Spray a 9x13 pan with cooking spray.
2. Mix all ingredients and bake in the pan at 350°F for 20 minutes.

## Notes

- Can also use a red velvet, chocolate, or birthday cake mix.
- Use different types of chips or add nuts.
- Use holiday chips for seasonal treats.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1/2 lb. ground pork, or another 1/2 lb. beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 medium head cabbage, cored and chopped
- 3/4 cup uncooked long grain white rice
- 1 can (28 oz.) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (10.75 oz.) tomato soup
- 1 cup water
- 1 Tbsp brown sugar

## Directions

1. Brown the beef and pork with the onion in a skillet. Drain the fat, then stir in the garlic, salt, and pepper.
2. Mix the crushed tomatoes, tomato soup, water, and brown sugar in a bowl.
3. In the crock pot, layer half the cabbage, all the meat, the rice, then the rest of the cabbage.
4. Pour the tomato mixture over the top.
5. Cook on low 6-8 hours, until the cabbage is tender and the rice is cooked. Stir before serving.

## Notes

- All the flavor of stuffed cabbage without rolling the leaves.
- Use regular long grain rice, not instant, or it turns to mush.

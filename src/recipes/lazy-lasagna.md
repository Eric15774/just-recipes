## Ingredients

- 2 cups spaghetti sauce
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 3 cups cooked wide egg noodles
- 2 Tbsp grated parmesan cheese
- Chopped fresh parsley, optional

## Directions

1. Warm the spaghetti sauce, stir in the mozzarella and cottage cheese. Fold in the noodles.
2. Pour into a greased casserole dish. Sprinkle with parmesan cheese.
3. Bake, uncovered, at 375°F until bubbly, about 30 minutes. If desired, top with chopped fresh parsley.

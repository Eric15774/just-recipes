## Ingredients

- Small box instant chocolate pudding
- 1 cup milk
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 1 cup Cool Whip

## Directions

1. Mix the pudding mix, milk, and powdered sugar.
2. After combined, fold in the Cool Whip.
3. Spread on cake.

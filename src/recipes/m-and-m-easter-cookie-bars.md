## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 1 1/2 cup graham cracker crumbs
- 1 14 oz can sweetened condensed milk
- 2 cups chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup quick oats
- 1/2 cup butterscotch chips
- 1 cup Easter M&M's

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Combine the melted butter and graham cracker crumbs in a bowl until crumbly.
3. Press into the bottom of a parchment-lined 9x13 baking dish.
4. Pour the sweetened condensed milk over the graham cracker layer.
5. Sprinkle with the oats, chocolate chips, butterscotch chips, and M&M's.
6. Bake for 25-30 minutes.

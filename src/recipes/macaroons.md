## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 5 1/2 cups flaked coconut
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line cookie sheets with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, stir together the flour, coconut, and salt. Stir in the sweetened condensed milk and vanilla using your hands until well blended.
3. Use an ice cream scoop to drop dough onto the prepared cookie sheets, about golf ball size.
4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the coconut is toasted.

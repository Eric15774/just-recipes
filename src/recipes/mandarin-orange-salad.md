## Ingredients

Salad:

- 1 large bowl of leaf lettuce
- 1 can mandarin oranges, drained
- 2/3 cup slivered almonds
- 1/2 cup onion, chopped

Dressing:

- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon dry mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1/4 cup plus 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 3/4 tablespoon poppy seeds

## Directions

1. Place the lettuce, oranges, almonds, and onion in a large bowl.
2. Combine the dressing ingredients in a jar and shake well.
3. Pour the dressing over the salad just before serving.

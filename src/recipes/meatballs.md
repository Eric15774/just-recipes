## Ingredients

- 2 lbs. lean ground beef
- 1 lb. ground pork

Add:

- 1/2 cup Italian style breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp salt for every pound of meat
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp minced onion
- 1/2 tsp dried parsley
- 1 egg per pound of meat
- Garlic salt, sprinkled

## Directions

1. Mix with your hands. If it is too mushy, sprinkle in more breadcrumbs.
2. Cook in sauce.

## Notes

- The original gives no cooking time. Pair with [Spaghetti Sauce](/recipes/spaghetti-sauce/) if you like.

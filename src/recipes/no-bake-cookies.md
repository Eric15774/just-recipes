## Ingredients

- 2 sticks butter
- 4 cups sugar
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 6 Tbsp cocoa
- 1 Tbsp vanilla
- 6 c. quick rolled oats

## Directions

1. Combine the butter, sugar, milk, peanut butter, and cocoa. Bring to a slow boil for five minutes.
2. Add the vanilla and quick rolled oats.
3. Spoon onto waxed paper and let cool.

## Ingredients

- 1 package egg free noodles
- 2 cups TVP
- 2 Tbsp beef-like seasoning
- 1 Tbsp Bragg's liquid aminos or soy sauce
- 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 1/8 cup chopped onions
- 1 cup pasta sauce
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp minced garlic

## Directions

1. Cook the noodles.
2. Cook the rest of the ingredients in a saucepan.
3. Mix the noodles with the sauce and eat. Yum!

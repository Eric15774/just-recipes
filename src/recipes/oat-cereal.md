## Ingredients

- 2-3 Tbsp natural peanut butter
- 1/2 Tbsp honey
- 1/4 cup dry quick oats
- Soymilk, for serving

## Directions

1. Stir together the peanut butter and honey.
2. Add the quick oats and mix well.
3. Serve with soymilk.

## Ingredients

- 2 c. warm water
- 1/4 c. Bragg's liquid aminos (or low sodium soy sauce)
- 1 T. oil
- 1/2 T. onion powder
- 1 T. honey
- 1 t. sage
- 2 T. nutritional yeast flakes
- 1/4 t. garlic powder
- 1/4 t. Italian seasoning
- 2 c. rolled oats

## Directions

1. Combine the first nine ingredients and bring to a boil.
2. Add the oats and cook 5 minutes.
3. Form small patties and bake on a sprayed cookie sheet at 350°F for 12 to 15 minutes on each side.

## Notes

- I scoop out piles onto the cookie sheet and then pat them flat and round, like patties, with a rubber spatula.
- Used in [Vegan Breakfast Casserole](/recipes/vegan-breakfast-casserole/).

## Ingredients

Crust:

- 4 c. oats
- 1 c. whole wheat flour
- 1 c. coconut, optional
- 1/2 c. chopped almonds
- 1 t. vanilla
- 1 c. apple juice

Filling:

- 6-7 large apples
- 12 oz. can apple juice concentrate
- 1/4 c. cornstarch
- 1/2 t. coriander and cardamom (or cinnamon)
- 1/2 c. water

## Directions

1. Mix the crust dry ingredients. Add the apple juice and stir. Press half of the crust into the bottom of a sprayed 9x13 pan.
2. Peel, core, and slice the apples.
3. Thicken the remaining filling ingredients on the stove. Add the apples and fold in gently.
4. Pour the filling into the crust and put the rest of the crust on top.
5. Bake at 350°F for approximately 30 minutes.

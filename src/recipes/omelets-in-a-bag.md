A great idea when you are entertaining family and friends for breakfast. Everyone gets involved in the process, and it is a great conversation piece.

## Ingredients

- Quart size freezer bags, one per guest
- Permanent marker
- 2 eggs per bag (large or extra-large, not more than 2)
- Fillings of choice, such as cheese, ham, onion, green pepper, tomato, hash browns, salsa

## Directions

1. Give each guest a quart size freezer bag and have them write their name on it with a permanent marker.
2. Crack 2 eggs into each bag and shake to combine.
3. Put out a variety of prepared fillings. Each guest adds their choices to the bag and shakes. Get the air out of the bag and zip it up.
4. Place the bags into rolling boiling water for exactly 13 minutes, or a little longer. You can usually cook 6-8 omelets in a large pot. For more, make another pot of boiling water.
5. Open the bags and the omelet will roll out easily.

## Notes

- Be prepared for everyone to be amazed.
- Nice to serve with fresh fruit and coffee cake.

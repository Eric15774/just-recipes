## Ingredients

- 1 small box orange Jell-O
- 1 small box instant vanilla pudding
- 1 cup boiling water
- 1/2 cup cold water
- 1 (8 oz) container Cool Whip
- 1 (14 oz) can mandarin oranges, drained
- 1 cup mini marshmallows

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, combine the orange Jell-O and boiling water. Whisk until dissolved. Add the cold water and chill in the refrigerator for 15 minutes.
2. Slowly whisk in the vanilla pudding. Mix until smooth and chill another 15-20 minutes, or until slightly thickened.
3. Fold in the Cool Whip, mandarin oranges, and marshmallows.

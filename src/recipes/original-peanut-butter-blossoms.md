## Ingredients

- 3 1/2 cups flour
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup milk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla
- Milk chocolate candy kisses

## Directions

1. Sift together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
2. Cream the butter, sugar, peanut butter, and brown sugar.
3. Add the eggs, milk, and vanilla.
4. Sift in the dry ingredients and mix well.
5. Shape into balls. Bake at 350°F for about 8 minutes.
6. Remove from the oven and place a solid milk chocolate candy kiss on top, then put back into the oven for a few minutes.

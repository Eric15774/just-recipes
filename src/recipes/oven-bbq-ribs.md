## Ingredients

- 2 slabs pork or beef ribs
- 2 tbs seasoning salt
- 16 oz BBQ sauce

## Directions

1. Wash the ribs. Place in a large roasting pan or broiling pan lined with aluminum foil.
2. Sprinkle with seasoning salt and smother with BBQ sauce.
3. Cover the pan tightly with aluminum foil or a tight fitting lid.
4. Place in a 350°F oven for 4 hours.

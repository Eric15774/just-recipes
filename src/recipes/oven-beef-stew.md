## Ingredients

- 2 pounds stewing beef, cut in cubes
- 3-4 medium potatoes, pared and quartered
- 6 to 8 medium carrots, pared and cut
- 1/2 cup celery
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 3 Tablespoons tapioca (quick cooking)
- 1 lb. can tomatoes, cut up
- 1 Tablespoon parsley
- 2 1/2 teaspoons salt, 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 cup beef broth, or 1 cup water and 1 bouillon cube

## Directions

1. Brown the beef cubes. Add the remaining ingredients and place in a large casserole.
2. Cover and bake in a 350°F oven for 2 hours.
3. Remove the bay leaf before serving.

## Notes

- I always buy packaged stew meat, which is already cubed.

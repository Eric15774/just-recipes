## Ingredients

- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp baking powder
- 1 cup butter, cold
- 2 beaten eggs
- 1 1/3 cup light cream or half and half

## Directions

1. Mix the dry ingredients together. Cut in the butter to form coarse crumbles (do not over mix).
2. Combine the eggs and cream in a separate bowl, then stir into the dry mix.
3. Spread into 2 greased 8-inch rounds or a greased 13x9 pan, building up the edges.
4. Bake at 450°F for about 15 minutes. Cool 5 minutes before cutting.

## Notes

- Can slice into layers and pour strawberries and sugar mix over it, or eat plain. Best served warm.

## Ingredients

Option 1:

- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup butter
- 2 lb. bag confectioners' sugar
- Melted chocolate, for dipping

Option 2:

- 2/3 cup peanut butter
- 4-5 cups powdered sugar
- 1 cup butter
- 2 cups chocolate chips
- 3/4 bar paraffin

## Directions

**Option 1:** Mix the ingredients using your hands. Form into balls or eggs and chill. Dip into melted chocolate.

**Option 2:** Mix the peanut butter, powdered sugar, and butter well, using your hands. Form into balls and chill. Melt the chocolate chips and paraffin (or use melting chocolate instead). Dip the balls into the chocolate mixture and chill.

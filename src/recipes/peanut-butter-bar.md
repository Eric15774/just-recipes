## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cup smooth peanut butter
- 1 1/2 cup graham cracker crumbs
- 1 stick melted margarine
- 1 3/4 cup powdered sugar
- 1 pkg (6 oz) chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Mix all ingredients except the chocolate chips. Spread in a 9x13 pan.
2. Bake at 350°F for 8 minutes.
3. Spread the melted chocolate chips to cover the top. Cool until the chocolate hardens. Cut into squares. Freeze.

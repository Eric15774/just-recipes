## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup peanut butter
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Hershey's Kisses

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
3. In a large bowl, cream together the peanut butter, sugar, and melted butter until well combined.
4. Beat in the egg, milk, and vanilla extract.
5. Gradually stir in the dry ingredients until a soft dough forms.
6. Roll the dough into 1-inch balls and place on an ungreased baking sheet.
7. Bake for 10-12 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden.
8. Press a Hershey's Kiss into the center with a slight twisting motion as soon as removed from the oven.

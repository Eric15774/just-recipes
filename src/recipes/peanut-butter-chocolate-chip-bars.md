## Ingredients

- 1 box yellow cake mix
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1 cup crunchy peanut butter
- 12 oz chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Grease and flour a jelly roll pan.
2. Beat the eggs, water, butter, crunchy peanut butter, and half of the cake mix. Beat well, then add the rest of the cake mix.
3. Add the chocolate chips.
4. Spread in the pan and bake 20-30 minutes at 375°F, until golden brown.

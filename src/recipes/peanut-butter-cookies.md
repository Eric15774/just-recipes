## Ingredients

- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tsp baking soda

## Directions

1. Mix all together, roll the mixture into small balls.
2. Bake on a greased cookie sheet for 10 minutes at 350°F.

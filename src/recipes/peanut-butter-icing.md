## Ingredients

- 1 stick butter
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1 small container Cool Whip

## Directions

1. Melt the butter on low heat. Add the brown sugar. Stir well. Remove from heat.
2. Add the peanut butter and stir. Cool completely.
3. Add the Cool Whip, stir until thoroughly mixed.

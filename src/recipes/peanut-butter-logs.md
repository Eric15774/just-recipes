## Ingredients

- 1 cup honey
- 1 cup dry milk
- 1 cup peanut butter
- Sprinkles, chocolate chips, nuts, etc., for rolling

## Directions

1. Mix the honey, dry milk, and peanut butter.
2. Shape into 2 logs or into 2-inch logs and roll in the topping of choice.
3. Refrigerate until hard. If making large logs, slice before serving.

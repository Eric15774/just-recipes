## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 egg
- 1 1/4 cup flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Miniature peanut butter cups

## Directions

1. Cream together the sugar, butter, peanut butter, brown sugar, vanilla, and egg.
2. Add the flour, salt, and baking soda.
3. Roll into balls or drop a small scoopful into a tassie pan.
4. Bake at 350°F for 8-10 minutes.
5. Immediately after pulling from the oven, press a miniature peanut butter cup into the center of each cookie.

## Ingredients

Cookies:

- 1 cup butter, softened
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup crushed peppermint candy
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 cups flour
- 1/4 cup cornstarch

Frosting:

- 1 cup confectioners' sugar
- 1-2 Tbsp milk
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 2 Tbsp crushed peppermint candy

## Directions

1. Mix the butter, sugar, crushed candy, and vanilla thoroughly using a mixer. Gradually blend in the flour and cornstarch.
2. Form into 1 inch balls and place on baking sheets. Gently press down each cookie to flatten using fingers or a flat-bottomed drinking glass (dipped in sugar to prevent sticking).
3. Bake at 300°F for 25-30 minutes. Cool completely.
4. Combine the confectioners' sugar, milk, and vanilla. Mix until the frosting is smooth.
5. Drizzle the cookies with frosting and sprinkle with crushed candy.

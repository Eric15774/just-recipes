## Ingredients

Bottom layer:

- 4 Tbsp pineapple juice
- 1 1/2 Tbsp olive oil
- 4 Tbsp sucanat/sugar
- Pineapple slices

Batter:

- 2 1/2 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 Tbsp baking powder
- 1 1/2 cup juice and water
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 cup sucanat/sugar

## Directions

1. Put the pineapple juice, olive oil, and sucanat/sugar in the bottom of the pan, then place the pineapple slices on top.
2. In a separate bowl, mix the flour, salt, and baking powder.
3. Add the juice and water, olive oil, vanilla, and sucanat/sugar to the dry ingredients and mix.
4. Pour the batter over the pineapple. Bake at 350°F for 35-40 minutes.

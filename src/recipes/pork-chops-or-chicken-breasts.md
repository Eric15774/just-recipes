## Ingredients

- Zesty Italian salad dressing
- Italian breadcrumbs
- Parmesan cheese

## Directions

1. Coat the meat with zesty Italian salad dressing.
2. Mix the Italian breadcrumbs and parmesan cheese together. Coat the meat with the breadcrumb mixture.
3. Place in a baking dish and cover with foil.
4. Bake at 350°F for about 1 hour.

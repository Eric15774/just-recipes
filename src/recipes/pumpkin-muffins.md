## Ingredients

- 1 box spice cake mix
- 1 small can pumpkin
- Chocolate chips (dark preferred)

## Directions

1. Mix the cake mix and pumpkin together until smooth.
2. Add chocolate chips to taste.
3. Scoop into cupcake liners or a greased cupcake pan.
4. Bake at 350°F for 20-24 minutes.

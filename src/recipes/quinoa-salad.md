## Ingredients

- 3 cups quinoa (okay to add extra)
- 2 tsp granulated onion powder
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 2-3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 (16 oz) can olives, cut in half lengthwise
- 1 bunch green onions, sliced (about 8)
- 1 small head Romaine, chopped
- 1 small cucumber, diced
- 1 pint cherry or grape tomatoes, halved (about 30)
- 2 radishes, minced, optional
- 1 recipe [Creamy Vegan Italian Dressing](/recipes/creamy-vegan-italian-dressing/)

## Directions

1. Stir the first four ingredients together (the quinoa, onion powder, basil, and oregano).
2. Add the garlic and olives.
3. When cool, add the vegetables.
4. Drizzle on 1 recipe of Creamy Vegan Italian Dressing. Stir and serve.

## Notes

- The original does not say whether the quinoa is measured cooked or dry, and has no cooking instructions.

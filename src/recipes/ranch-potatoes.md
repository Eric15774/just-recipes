## Ingredients

- 1 packet ranch dip mix
- Olive oil
- Potatoes, cubed into 1 inch cubes

## Directions

1. Toss the potatoes in olive oil, then toss with the ranch dip mix.
2. Bake on a cookie tray or stone in a 375°F oven until soft.

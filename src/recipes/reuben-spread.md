## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound corned beef, shredded or chopped
- 1 (16 oz) can sauerkraut, well drained
- 1-2 cups shredded Swiss cheese
- 1-2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- Snack rye bread
- Thousand Island dressing

## Directions

1. Combine all the ingredients except the bread and dressing in a slow cooker. Mix well.
2. Cover and cook on high 1 to 2 hours, until heated through, stirring occasionally.
3. Turn to low and keep warm in the cooker while serving.
4. Put the spread on bread slices. Top individual servings with Thousand Island dressing.

## Ingredients

- 12 green tomatoes
- 6 green peppers
- 6 red peppers
- 12 small onions
- 1/2 cup salt
- 4 cups sugar
- 2 Tbsp celery seed
- 1 Tbsp mustard seed
- 3 cups vinegar

## Directions

1. Grind together the tomatoes, peppers, onions, and salt. Let stand overnight.
2. The next day, drain and rinse well.
3. Add the sugar, celery seed, mustard seed, and vinegar.
4. Bring to a boil and boil 5 minutes.
5. Seal hot in jars.

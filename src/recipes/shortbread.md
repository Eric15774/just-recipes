## Ingredients

- 5 1/2 cups flour (680 grams)
- 1 cup sugar (230 grams)
- 1 cup softened butter (454 grams)

## Directions

1. Mix the ingredients together until thoroughly combined but not overworked.
2. Press into a baking pan like a 9x13 pan or smaller ones as needed (a cutter can also make 1" round bites, or use molds if available). The dough should be pressed or rolled to 1/2 to 3/4 inch thick.
3. Bake 25 to 30 minutes at 375°F, do not let them brown, then immediately sprinkle with sugar.
4. Cool in the pan or on a rack.

## Notes

- Store in an airtight container at room temperature, or freeze for a future event.

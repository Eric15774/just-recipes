## Ingredients

- 1 lb. Great Northern beans, sorted and rinsed
- 6 cups water
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/2 tsp salt (or less)

## Directions

1. Place everything in a sprayed crockpot and cook on low about 8 hours (give or take, depending on your crockpot).
2. Leave on warm until ready to eat. Mmm.

Three combinations, with amounts left to you.

## Ingredients

Carob and peanut butter:

- Frozen bananas*
- Natural peanut butter
- Carob powder
- Soymilk
- Carob chips (optional, add in the last few seconds)

Orange and mango:

- Frozen bananas*
- Frozen mango chunks
- Orange juice (or a scoop of OJ concentrate and water)

Strawberry, pineapple, and banana:

- Frozen bananas*
- Frozen strawberries
- Pineapple (canned, fresh, or frozen)
- Orange juice (or concentrate and water)

## Directions

1. Blend the amounts you want, with as much liquid as you like, from thick and spoonable to thin and drinkable.
2. Blend just until smooth enough.

## Notes

- *Peel bananas, break them in chunks, and freeze in a Ziploc bag.

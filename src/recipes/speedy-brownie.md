## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 1 3/4 cups flour
- 1/2 cup cocoa
- 1 tsp. salt
- 5 eggs
- 1 cup vegetable (or canola) oil
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips

## Directions

1. Mix the sugar, flour, cocoa, salt, eggs, oil, and vanilla. Beat until smooth.
2. Pour into a greased 9x13x2 inch pan. Sprinkle with the chocolate chips.
3. Bake in a 350°F oven for 30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
4. Cool in the pan or on a wire rack.

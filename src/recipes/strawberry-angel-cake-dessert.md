## Ingredients

- 6 oz strawberry Jello
- 16 oz frozen strawberries
- 9" angel food cake
- 2 cups hot water

## Directions

1. In a large mixing bowl, dissolve the jello in the hot water. Add the frozen strawberries, stirring until the berries are thawed and the jello is syrupy.
2. Press the cake into the jello mixture. Cover the bowl and tip it to coat the cake thoroughly.
3. Refrigerate 2 hours before serving.
4. Let sit at room temperature at least 15 min, then invert onto a 10" cake taker, frost with Cool Whip, and enjoy.

## Notes

- I use a large Tupperware mixing bowl with a seal for this. You can buy an angel food cake, or make your own from a mix.

## Ingredients

Crust:

- 1 1/2 cups crushed pretzels
- 4 1/2 tbsp white sugar
- 3/4 cup melted butter

Cream cheese layer:

- 1 cup white sugar
- 2 (8 oz) cream cheese
- 1 (8 oz) frozen whipped topping

Strawberry layer:

- 1 (6 oz) strawberry jello
- 2 cups boiling water
- 1 (16 oz) frozen strawberries

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Mix the pretzels, 4 1/2 tbsp sugar, and butter in a 9x13 pan. Bake until toasted. Cool completely.
2. Beat the 1 cup sugar and the cream cheese until smooth. Beat in the whipped topping. Spread evenly over the crust and refrigerate until set, about 30 minutes.
3. Stir together the jello and boiling water. Mix in the frozen strawberries until thawed. Pour over the cream cheese mixture.
4. Refrigerate until set, about 1-2 hours, before serving.

## Notes

- The original gives no baking time for the crust, only "until toasted."

## Ingredients

- 2 cups oats
- 1 cup flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp cardamom
- 1 tsp coriander (or cinnamon in place of these spices)
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 1 1/4 cup water
- 1/4 c oil
- 1 cup raisins

## Directions

1. Mix the dry ingredients and wet ingredients separately, then combine. Add the raisins.
2. Bake 15 minutes at 350°F or 12 minutes at 375°F.

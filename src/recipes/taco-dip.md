## Ingredients

- 2 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
- 8 ounces sour cream
- Dry taco seasoning, to suit your taste
- 1 jar salsa (I used mild)
- Shredded cheese (cheddar or a taco blend)

## Directions

1. Mix the sour cream, cream cheese, and seasoning with a mixer. Spread on a plate.
2. Spoon the salsa over the top of that, then sprinkle the shredded cheese over the salsa.

## Notes

- I don't use a certain amount of salsa or shredded cheese. Just judge what you need to cover the mixture.
- You can also chop lettuce and tomato and use them in place of the salsa.

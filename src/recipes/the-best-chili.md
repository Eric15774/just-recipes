## Ingredients

- Vegan ground beef substitute (optional)
- 2 c. chopped onion
- 2 T. finely chopped garlic
- 4 T. chili powder
- 1 t. oregano
- 1 t. cumin
- 1 t. pepper flakes
- 6 oz. tomato paste
- 4 c. water with 4 vegetable bouillon cubes
- 1 T. brown sugar
- 1 t. salt
- 1 (28 oz) can red beans

## Directions

1. Cook the onions and garlic, and the ground beef substitute if using.
2. Add the rest and simmer for a good long time. Make sure the bouillon cubes dissolve.

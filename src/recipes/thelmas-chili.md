## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef
- 1/2 cup chopped onions
- 1/3 cup chopped peppers
- Garlic powder or dried garlic
- 1 16 oz can kidney beans
- 1 14 oz can tomatoes
- 1 8 oz can tomato sauce, if desired
- 2 T. A-1 steak sauce
- 2 T. chili powder
- 1/4 tsp. salt

## Directions

1. Brown the meat; add the onion, peppers, and garlic. Cook until the meat is browned and the onions and peppers are tender.
2. Stir in the remaining ingredients.
3. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer until done, stirring occasionally.

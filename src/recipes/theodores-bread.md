## Ingredients

- 3-4 cups whole wheat flour
- 1 Tbsp yeast
- 2 Tbsp soy flour (or gluten flour)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 Tbsp oil
- 1 Tbsp honey
- 1/3 cup raisins, optional

## Directions

1. Mix the dry ingredients and the liquid ingredients separately.
2. Mix the two together and knead until smooth.
3. Shape into a loaf. Let rise to double.
4. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes.

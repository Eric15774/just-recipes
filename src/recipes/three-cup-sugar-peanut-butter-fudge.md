## Ingredients

- 3 cups sugar
- 3/4 cup margarine
- 2/3 cup evaporated milk
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 (7 oz) jar marshmallow cream
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions

1. Combine the sugar, margarine, and milk. Bring to a rolling boil, stirring constantly. Boil 5 minutes over medium heat.
2. Remove from heat and stir in the peanut butter until well blended.
3. Add the marshmallow cream and vanilla. Beat until well blended.
4. Pour into a greased 13x9 pan.

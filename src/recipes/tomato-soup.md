## Ingredients

Full recipe (makes 14 to 16 quarts):

- 100-120 tomatoes
- 7 onions, quartered
- 12 stems celery, cut into pieces
- 21 whole cloves
- 14 sprigs parsley

Half recipe (makes 7 to 8 quarts):

- 50-60 tomatoes
- 3 1/2 onions, quartered
- 6 stems celery, cut into pieces
- 11 whole cloves
- 7 sprigs parsley

For the full recipe, add:

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup flour
- 2 Tablespoons salt

For the half recipe, add:

- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 Tablespoon salt

## Directions

1. Cook the tomatoes, onions, celery, cloves, and parsley, then strain through a food mill.
2. Stir in the sugar, black pepper, butter, and flour for the batch size you're making.
3. Let boil, then jar and seal.

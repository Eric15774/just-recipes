## Ingredients

Mash in a bowl:

- 1 package firm tofu (less than 15 oz), rinsed and drained
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp poultry seasoning (or chicken-like seasoning)

Then stir in:

- 3-4 cups unsweetened soymilk
- 4 slices bread, diced
- 12 [Oat Sausage](/recipes/oat-sausage/) patties, diced

## Directions

1. Spray a 9x13 dish. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mash the tofu, salt, turmeric, and poultry seasoning in a bowl.
3. Stir in the soymilk, bread, and diced oat sausage patties.
4. Pour into the 9x13 dish and bake for 30 minutes.

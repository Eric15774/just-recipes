## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup plus 2 Tbsp flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 cup flaked coconut
- 1/2 cup chopped cashews
- 1/2 cup mini chocolate chips
- Confectioners' sugar, optional

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, combine the sugars, egg, butter, and vanilla.
2. Combine the flour, salt, and baking soda; gradually add to the sugar mixture. Stir in the coconut, cashews, and chocolate chips.
3. Drop batter by tablespoonfuls 1 in. apart onto a preheated waffle iron coated with cooking spray.
4. Bake for 1-2 minutes, or until golden brown. Carefully remove to wire racks; cool completely.
5. Dust with confectioners' sugar if desired.

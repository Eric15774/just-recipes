## Ingredients

- 1 (12 oz) can apple juice concentrate
- 1 (12 oz) can cranberry juice concentrate
- 1 (6 oz) can orange juice concentrate
- 6 (12 oz) cans water
- 2 t. coriander and cardamom (or cinnamon)

## Directions

1. Pour all the ingredients into a large pot.
2. Simmer 30 minutes until hot. Stir and serve.

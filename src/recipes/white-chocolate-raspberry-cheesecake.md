## Ingredients

Crust:

- 1 c. graham cracker crumbs
- 3 tbsp. sugar
- 1/4 c. melted butter

Cake:

- 1 can raspberry pie filling
- 2 c. white chocolate chips
- 1/2 c. half and half
- 3 (8 oz.) cream cheese
- 1/2 c. sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Mix the crust ingredients together and press into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan (bottom of pan may be lined with parchment paper). Bake 10-15 min. Cool.

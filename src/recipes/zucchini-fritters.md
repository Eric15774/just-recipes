## Ingredients

- 2 cups grated zucchini
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 1/2 cup Romano cheese
- 3/4 cup flour
- 1 teaspoon parsley
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- Oil, for frying

## Directions

1. Mix all the ingredients except the zucchini, then add the zucchini.
2. Fry in hot oil like a pancake.
